## Region notes

### Hanoi — 2–3 nights
The base and the palate-setter. On arrival day you want to do nothing but eat. On a full
day you can walk: Hoan Kiem → St Joseph's Cathedral → Train Street (see the hack in
`food-and-experiences.md`) → Dong Xuan market → Long Bien Bridge at sunset → Ta Hien
"beer corner" at night. Christmas Day in the Old Quarter is genuinely atmospheric —
Hanoi is not a Christian city, so it's a normal working day with fairy lights, not a
shutdown. **NYE is the one night Hanoi fills up:** midnight fireworks over Hoan Kiem Lake
(the city also puts a stage near the Opera House and at Thong Nhat Park). Source:
GetYourGuide's Hanoi NYE explainer (https://www.getyourguide.com/explorer/hanoi-ttd205/new-years-eve-in-hanoi/,
checked 2026-10-07 — **commercial, but consistent with local reports**).

### Ninh Binh — 1–2 nights
The easiest big win on the trip: 2 hours, no flights, a boat ride through caves, and the
best dawn photograph you'll take without a motorbike. Two days lets you do Trang An (the
longer, better boat route) on day one and Hang Mua + Tam Coc + cycling on day two.
One night is enough if you arrive early. **Stay in Tam Coc, not Ninh Binh city** — the
city is a transit town, Tam Coc is in the karst.
Entrance/boat prices are in `costs.md` (Trang An boat ~250,000 VND; Mua Cave quoted
between 100,000 and 200,000 VND across sources — flagged as uncertain there).

### Ha Giang — 3 nights (the reason to come)
This is the trip's centrepiece and the answer to "motorbiking + trekking/homestays +
photography" in one go. The classic route runs Ha Giang city → Quan Ba (Heaven's Gate)
→ Yen Minh → Dong Van → **Ma Pi Leng Pass** → Meo Vac → back via Du Gia. Lonely Planet's
itinerary breaks it as Leg 1 Ha Giang–Yen Minh, Leg 2 Yen Minh–Dong Van, Leg 3 Dong Van–
Du Gia, Leg 4 Du Gia–Ha Giang
(https://www.lonelyplanet.com/articles/ultimate-itinerary-ha-giang-loop-vietnam, checked
2026-10-07). A 3-day/2-night version is the standard minimum; **4 days/3 nights adds Du
Gia village and its waterfalls** and is the version most operators recommend if you have
the time (https://hagiangmotorventures.com/blogs/best-ha-giang-loop-tour-companies-recommendations,
checked 2026-10-07 — **operator, self-interested, but the 3-vs-4-day trade-off is
consistent across operators**).

**Get there the night before or accept a 06:00 arrival.** The overnight sleeper bus
lands you at 04:30–06:00; that is either the start of loop day 1 or a wasted morning.

**Do the loop with an easy rider, not self-ride.** This is not a nervous-traveller
recommendation — it's a legal one. See `transport.md`: **Vietnam recognises only 1968
Vienna Convention IDPs, and Singapore's Automobile Association issues 1949 Convention
IDPs which AA Singapore itself says Vietnam does not recognise.** Self-riding a
>50cc bike is therefore not legal for them, and insurance will very likely not pay out.
An easy rider (a local licensed driver on his own bike, you as pillion) removes the
legal problem and the mountain-riding risk in one move.

### Why I'm excluding Sapa, Ha Long/Cat Ba and Cao Bang
- **Sapa** has the worst weather profile of any candidate (~7°C days, fog, frost, mud)
  and duplicates Ha Giang's scenery. `weather.md` has the sources.
- **Ha Long / Cat Ba** is the one place where the weather can take away the thing you
  paid for: fog and winter wind are explicitly among the reasons cruises get cancelled.
- **Cao Bang / Ban Gioc** is beautiful and I'd send someone there, but it's a 4–5 day
  loop of its own, 7–9 hours from Hanoi, and the waterfall is at dry-season low in
  December. It cannot fit alongside Hanoi *and* Ninh Binh *and* a 3-day loop.

## ⭐ PROPOSED ROUTING — 8 nights / 9 days

**Hanoi (2) → Ha Giang loop (3) → Ninh Binh (2) → Hanoi for NYE (1)**

| Night | Date | Where | Day in one line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Thu 24 Dec | Hanoi | Land Noi Bai, taxi to Old Quarter, first bia hoi and pho. Nothing else. |
| 2 | Fri 25 Dec | Hanoi | Christmas Day: Old Quarter on foot, egg coffee, Long Bien sunset. **21:00 overnight bus to Ha Giang.** |
| 3 | Sat 26 Dec | Ha Giang (homestay) | Arrive ~05:00, loop **day 1**: Ha Giang → Quan Ba Heaven's Gate → Yen Minh. |
| 4 | Sun 27 Dec | Ha Giang (homestay) | Loop **day 2**: Yen Minh → Dong Van → **Ma Pi Leng Pass** → Meo Vac. The money day. |
| 5 | Mon 28 Dec | Ha Giang town | Loop **day 3**: Meo Vac → Du Gia → Ha Giang. Hot shower, thang co, sleep. |
| 6 | Tue 29 Dec | Ninh Binh (Tam Coc) | Transit day: Ha Giang → Hanoi → Ninh Binh, ~9–10 h door to door. Arrive late afternoon, sunset over the paddies. |
| 7 | Wed 30 Dec | Ninh Binh (Tam Coc) | Full day: Trang An boat, Hang Mua climb, cycling, goat meat dinner. |
| 8 | Thu 31 Dec | Hanoi | Morning boat or Mua Cave sunrise → back to Hanoi by ~16:00 → **NYE fireworks at Hoan Kiem**. |
| — | Fri 1 Jan | — | Fly SIN. (Add a 9th night to 2 Jan if you want a softer finish.) |

### Why this shape and not the alternatives
- **Two bases would be cleaner, three is worth the one hard day.** The only genuinely
  punishing leg is night 6 (Ha Giang → Ninh Binh, ~9–10 h). Everything else is under
  2.5 h. If that day looks unbearable, **cut Ninh Binh and give the nights to Hanoi**
  (making it Hanoi 2 + Ha Giang 3 + Hanoi 3) — you lose the karst and gain two slow
  mornings.
- **Ninh Binh comes after Ha Giang deliberately.** Coming off three cold, foggy mountain
  days and a night bus, a mild 20°C boat ride through a river cave is the recovery day.
  Doing it the other way round (Ninh Binh → Ha Giang) means the trip *ends* on the hardest
  transit.
- **NYE in Hanoi, not on a bus.** The loop has to finish by 30 Dec for this to work; that
  is exactly why night 5 is Ha Giang town and night 6 is the transfer. Do not compress
  the loop to 2 days/1 night — you'd spend it on the bike.
- **Christmas is in Hanoi, on purpose.** 25 Dec in Ha Giang would be a cold homestay with
  no celebration; in Hanoi it's fairy lights, a full Old Quarter and a real dinner.
- **If dates shift:** the whole plan is date-flexible by ±2 days **except** the
  Christmas/NYE hotel bookings, which are the scarce thing. Tet 2027 is 6 Feb, so there
  is no holiday interference anywhere near this window.

### What I'd cut if only 8 days (7 nights) were possible
Drop Ninh Binh to one night (arrive 29 Dec, leave 30 Dec) and add the spare night to
Hanoi. You still see Trang An and Tam Coc; you just do them faster.

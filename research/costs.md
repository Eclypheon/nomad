## 2. Three honest daily budgets (per person)

**Excluding accommodation and intercity transport.** Food + local transport + entrance
fees + a few drinks.

| Tier | Per day | SGD | What it buys |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Frugal** | 400,000 | **S$19.70** | 3 street meals (pho/banh mi/com tam), 1 coffee, bia hoi, walking or one short Grab, 1 small entrance fee |
| **Comfortable** ← the realistic default | **750,000** | **S$37.00** | 3 proper meals including one sit-down restaurant dinner, 2 coffees, beers, Grab everywhere, entrance fees, a spa or a tour on some days |
| **Splurge** | 1,500,000 | **S$74.00** | Restaurant dinners with wine, taxis, private guides, daily spa, shopping |

Arithmetic for the comfortable tier:
pho/banh mi breakfast 60,000 + lunch 90,000 + sit-down dinner 200,000 + 2 coffees 70,000
+ 2 beers 30,000 + water 20,000 + Grab share (2 pax) 80,000 + entrance fees amortised
100,000 + incidentals 100,000 = **750,000 VND**.

## 4. Assumptions

1. **Two people sharing one room.** All accommodation is a twin/double, halved.
2. **Hanoi mid-range, not luxury:** ~2,400,000 VND/room/night. If the Peridot class is
   sold out for Christmas and you're forced into the 3,000,000+ bracket, add ~S$60/person.
3. **Ha Giang is a 4-day/3-night easy-rider tour at US$225, all-inclusive** (bike, driver,
   fuel, border permit, homestays, all meals, boat ticket, entrance fees, protective gear).
   A 3D/2N tour would save ~US$25 and cost a day of the highlight.
4. **The loop's meals are included**, so 3 of the 8 days carry no food cost.
5. **Comfortable, not frugal and not splurge**, at 750,000 VND/day of on-the-ground spend.
6. **Trains and limousine vans, not private cars.** A private car Hanoi→Ha Giang is
   3,800,000–5,500,000 VND per vehicle — roughly +S$75–100/person versus the van.
7. **`regions.md` routing holds:** 8 nights, 3 bases, one hard transit day.
8. **Peak-season surcharge is not modelled above the guide prices.** The guide figures are
   already "typical", and the accommodation ones skew to the higher months — but a genuine
   Christmas/NYE hotel premium would add, not subtract.

## 5. Not included
- **International flights** (by definition of the budget).
- **Travel insurance** — shown separately; get a real quote.
- Visas — **not needed** for Singapore passports (`entry-and-logistics.md`).
- Souvenirs and shopping; alcohol beyond a few beers; spa beyond one massage.
- SIM/eSIM data (~S$10–20) — not sourced.
- Laundry, ATM/international card fees (Vietnamese ATMs typically charge ~50,000–70,000
  VND per withdrawal; **not sourced, treat as an estimate**).
- Any pre-departure costs: vaccinations, Japanese encephalitis / rabies series, IDP
  (which is pointless for Vietnam anyway — see `transport.md`).
- Medication, first-aid, travel adapters, and anything you'd buy to pack.

# Accommodation — shortlist per base

Researched 2026-10-07. **FX used throughout: 1 SGD = 20,285 VND** (open.er-api.com,
last updated 6 Oct 2026) and **1 USD ≈ 25,963 VND** for sources that quote in dollars.
SGD figures are conversions, not quotes.

Dates assumed (see `regions.md`; shift by ±2 days if the routing moves):

| Base | Check-in | Check-out | Nights |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hanoi (round 1) | 2026-12-24 | 2026-12-26 | 2 |
| Ha Giang | 2026-12-26 | 2026-12-29 | 3 |
| Ninh Binh (Tam Coc) | 2026-12-29 | 2026-12-31 | 2 |
| Hanoi (NYE) | 2026-12-31 | 2027-01-01 | 1 |

> **Booking urgency — read this first.** 24, 25 and 31 December in Hanoi is the tightest
> accommodation window of the Vietnamese year. The Old Quarter's good mid-range properties
> sell out for those specific nights and price 30–50% above low season; one guide puts a
> 3★ Hanoi boutique at **US$90–125/night in Nov–Dec versus $45–65 in Jun–Aug**
> (https://vietnamspot.ru/en/blog/hanoi-hotels, checked 2026-10-07). **Book Hanoi and NYE
> first, within 2–4 weeks.** Tam Coc and Ha Giang are less acute but the holiday week is
> still busy — 4–8 weeks is comfortable.
>
> **Every price below is "typical" from 2026 guides and aggregators, not a quote for your
> exact dates.** No live availability or rate was checked for 24 Dec – 1 Jan. Treat them as
> a starting point and a sanity check on what you're quoted.

## 1. Hanoi — 2 nights (24–26 Dec), Old Quarter

The whole point of staying in the Old Quarter is that you walk everywhere: Hoan Kiem Lake,
Ta Hien "beer corner", the food streets around Hang Manh/Hang Ga, Dong Xuan market and St
Joseph's Cathedral are all inside a 15-minute walk. Side-street rather than lake-front is
the sweet spot — quieter at night, still central.

| Property | Area | Why it's well located | Price/night | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hanoi La Siesta Premium Hang Be** | 27–29 Hang Be St, Hoan Kiem | 300 m from Hoan Kiem Lake, quiet inner courtyard, own spa | US$90–130 → **~2,860,000 VND / S$141** | The splurge; consistently a top-rated property in the city |
| **Hanoi Emerald Waters Hotel & Spa** | 38 Lo Su St, Hoan Kiem | Walk to the lake and the Old Quarter's food streets; rooftop pool | US$65–150 → **~2,790,000 VND / S$138** | Couples wanting a pool and a solid breakfast |
| **Peridot Grand Hotel & Spa** | Old Quarter | Modern 2022 interior, rooftop over the rooftops | US$75–110 → **~2,400,000 VND / S$118** | Best mid-range-value pick |
| **O'Gallery Premier Hotel & Spa** | 5 min walk to Hoan Kiem | Boutique format, free fruit bar | US$80–120 → **~2,600,000 VND / S$128** | Boutique feel without the La Siesta price |
| **Hanoi Tirant Hotel** | Old Quarter | Rooftop bar, central | US$50–90 → **~1,820,000 VND / S$90** | Good value, still 4★-ish |
| **Hanoi Pearl Hotel** | Old Quarter, central | Skyline rooftop bar | US$60–100 → **~2,080,000 VND / S$102** | Solid, unexciting, well located |
| **Essence Hanoi Hotel & Spa** | Old Quarter | Free breakfast, very central | US$60–110 → **~2,210,000 VND / S$109** | Reliable mid-range |
| **La Siesta Classic Ma May** | Ma May St | The family-friendly sister of the Premium | US$70–130 → **~2,600,000 VND / S$128** | Same group, slightly lower price point |

Price sources: https://hanoitourism.org/old-quarter-accommodation and
https://vietnamspot.ru/en/blog/hanoi-hotels and
https://familytravelpath.com/blog/where-to-stay-hanoi-neighborhoods (all checked
2026-10-07 — **travel-site aggregations, not hotel quotes**). Budget/backpacker context:
hostel dorms $7–15, simple private rooms $20–40, boutique 3–4★ $40–90, lakeside luxury

**For the 1-night NYE stay (31 Dec)** use the same shortlist — but note New Year's Eve
inventory is the scarcest of all. If the good mid-range is gone, an alternative is to
spend NYE in **Tam Coc** instead (see base 3), which is far quieter and cheaper, and
transfer back to Hanoi on 1 Jan for the flight. **This is a decision for Kester.**

## 2. Ninh Binh — 2 nights (29–31 Dec), Tam Coc

**Stay in Tam Coc or the Trang An corridor, not Ninh Binh city.** The city is a transit
town, 7–10 km from everything; Tam Coc is *in* the karst, walkable to the boat wharf and
restaurants, with bicycles usually thrown in free.

| Property | Area | Why it's well located | Price/night | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tam Coc Garden Resort** | Tam Coc | Bungalows in a real garden, unobstructed view of the Mua Cave karst range, pool, 15 min walk to the pier | US$35–55 → **~1,170,000 VND / S$58** | **Best all-round mid-range pick.** Photographers. |
| **Ninh Binh Hidden Charm Hotel** | Tam Coc / Trang An | Rooftop pool, 12 min walk to the pier | US$50–80 → **~1,690,000 VND / S$83** | Comfort-seekers; pool in December is a plus |
| **Tam Coc Rice Fields Resort** | Tam Coc | Boutique-luxury, garden setting, 20 min walk to pier | US$55–90 → **~1,880,000 VND / S$93** | The splurge option |
| **Trang An Retreat Homestay** | Trang An | Garden between karst formations, 1.5 km from the Trang An boats, 3 km from Hoa Lu | 450,000–650,000 VND → **~550,000 / S$27** | Couples + nature photographers |
| **Tam Coc Sunshine Homestay** | Tam Coc | Family-run, 6 rooms around a courtyard, **400 m from the boat wharf** | 300,000–450,000 VND → **~375,000 / S$18** | Value; breakfast (pho/banh cuon) included |
| **Nguyen Shack Tam Coc** | Tam Coc | 8 min walk to the pier | US$15–30 → **~580,000 VND / S$29** | Budget-social; still private rooms |

Price sources: https://bestofninhbinh.com/best-ninh-binh-hotels,
https://bestofninhbinh.com/ninh-binh-homestay-guide,
https://bestofninhbinh.com/where-to-stay-ninh-binh (all checked 2026-10-07 —
**aggregator/guide sites; treat as typical, not quoted**).
Note: December nights in Ninh Binh drop to ~12–15°C, so a heated pool or reliable hot
water is worth paying for.

## 3. Ha Giang — 3 nights (26–29 Dec)

This base splits in two: **1–2 nights in Ha Giang city** (before/after the loop) and
**2 nights of homestays on the loop itself**, which are almost always bundled into the
tour price — so the loop homestays are *not* separate bookings if you book a package.

### 3a. Ha Giang city (pre- and post-loop)

| Property | Area | Why it's well located | Price/night | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Yen Bien Luxury Hotel** | 517 Nguyen Trai St, town centre | 4★, 5-min walk to the Km 0 milestone, river views, rooftop pool | from 1,065,000 VND → **S$52** | The comfortable post-loop night — full reset before the transfer |
| **The Bluestone Lodge** | 239 Nguyen Van Linh St | Small boutique, rustic, friendly | from 550,000 VND → **S$27** | Value with character |
| **An's House** | Phuong Thien Commune | Out of town, garden setting | from 500,000 VND → **S$25** | Quiet, nature |
| **Truong Xuan Resort** | Quang Trung, edge of town | Eco setting, closest resort to town | US$19 → **~490,000 VND / S$24** | Cheap comfort |

Sources: https://vietdovetravel.com/the-best-homestays-and-hotels-in-ha-giang-for-2025
and https://wherebest.com/en/top10-hotels-ha-giang and
https://wherebest.com/en/ha-giang-where-to-stay (all checked 2026-10-07 — **aggregators**).
The city's mid-range hotels run ~600,000–1,200,000 VND (S$30–59); hostels/homestays from
100,000–300,000 VND.

### 3b. On the loop — homestays (usually inside the tour price)

Village homestays are traditional stilt houses, **100,000–200,000 VND/bed (S$5–10)** and
**usually include family-style dinner and breakfast** — which is why most operators bundle
them. Private rooms on the loop run 150,000–250,000 VND. Named examples (verify on the day
— these are small family operations):
- **Lo Lo Eco Lodge** — Lo Lo Chai, Dong Van. Closest stay to the Lung Cu flag tower. Dorms from ~US$8, earthen family rooms ~US$17, wooden bungalow with balcony ~US$25.
  (https://wherebest.com/en/top10-hotels-ha-giang, checked 2026-10-07; verified reachable)
  Booking page: https://www.agoda.com/khu-nghi-duong-h-mong-village/hotel/dong-van-vn.html
- **Dao Lodge Nam Dam** — Nam Dam village, Quan Ba. Green-architecture award winner. ~US$13.
- **Auberge de Meo Vac** — Chung Pua, Meo Vac. Century-old H'mong earthen house. ~US$14.
- **H'Mong Village Resort** — Quan Ba, ~15 min from Heaven's Gate. Mountain views + infinity pool; traditional double ~US$14, deluxe triple ~US$23.
  (https://www.agoda.com/khu-nghi-duong-h-mong-village/hotel/dong-van-vn.html)
- **Ancient Town Guesthouse** — 29 Pho Co St, **in the middle of Dong Van old town**, from 450,000 VND (S$22). (vietdovetravel, checked 2026-10-07)
- **Hoa Cuong Hotel Dong Van** — Dong Van old town, at the loop midpoint, most-reviewed stay in town, ~US$25.

**Practical note:** on a bookable 3-day/2-night loop you do **not** choose these — the
operator assigns them by route. Ask which ones you'll get, and confirm the rooms are
private rather than shared dorm if that matters.

### 3c. The splurge, if you want one
**P'apiu Resort** — P'apiu Hill, Bac Me (40 min from Ha Giang town), 5★, max 12 guests,
whole-resort privacy. **~US$550/night** (https://wherebest.com/en/top10-hotels-ha-giang,
checked 2026-10-07; booking page https://www.agoda.com/p-apiu-resort/hotel/ha-giang-vn.html,
verified reachable). Genuinely beautiful and genuinely out of budget — listed for
completeness rather than recommended.

## Booking-urgency summary

| Book | How urgent | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hanoi, 24–26 Dec | **Most urgent — within 2–4 weeks** | Christmas Eve/Day; Old Quarter sells out |
| Hanoi, 31 Dec (NYE) | **Most urgent — within 2–4 weeks** | NYE inventory is the scarcest of the window |
| Ha Giang loop + driver | **4–8 weeks** | Licensed easy riders sell out across the holiday week |
| Ha Giang city hotels, 26/28–29 Dec | 4–8 weeks | Smaller supply; the loop crowd competes for the same rooms |
| Ninh Binh / Tam Coc, 29–31 Dec | 4–6 weeks | Easier, but December is peak for Ninh Binh |
